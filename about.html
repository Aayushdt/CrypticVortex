About Evenly

At Evenly, we're passionate about making shared finances simple, fair, and transparent. Our journey began with a simple question: why is splitting expenses with friends still so complicated?

Our Mission

To create a world where friends and roommates never argue about money. We believe that financial transparency builds stronger relationships.

Our platform makes it effortless to track shared expenses, split bills fairly, and settle debts without the awkward money conversations.

Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a group of roommates who were tired of using spreadsheets to track shared expenses, Evenly has grown from a simple app to a comprehensive platform.

What started as a solution for ourselves has now helped thousands of users manage their group finances transparently.

Our Values

Simplicity - We believe financial tools should be accessible to everyone.

Transparency - We build trust through clear, open communication.

Fairness - We ensure everyone pays exactly what they owe, no more and no less.
